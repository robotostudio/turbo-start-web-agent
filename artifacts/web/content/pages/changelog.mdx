## 2026-09: the full redesign

The site's Blocks, design tokens and docs were reworked together. This entry lists what changed, and the last section lists what an existing site's content will notice.

### New Blocks

- `FeatureRows`: three full-width rows, each with a numbered title, a short body and an optional text link.
- `PreviewStage`: a heading and lede above an illustration of the site being edited in place.
- `FeaturedQuote`: one large quote with a person, and an optional company drawn beside it.

### Redesigned Blocks

- Hero, with a new `showcase` variant: copy above a textured band carrying a floating prompt card.
- LogoCloud, now a bordered ledger.
- Stats, now a ledger with an optional meter under each figure.
- Testimonial, with an optional featured quote and a highlighted phrase per quote.
- PostGrid and the /blog index, now cards with a cover image.
- CTA, on the dark textured band.
- ImageCards, in the same framed card PostGrid uses.
- Newsletter, on the textured band.
- Faq, using the browser's native disclosure, with an opening animation.
- FeatureGrid, a numbered ledger grid.
- FeatureSplit, with a framed image on a textured bed.
- Comparison, with a stacked layout on a phone.
- Gallery, three even rows of frames.
- Team, a ledger grid of portraits.
- Pricing, a ruled ledger of plans.

### System

- The eyebrow reversal: the small label above a heading now comes first, and the heading follows it.
- The type roles: each piece of text has a named role instead of a one-off size.
- New ledger, mock and subtle tokens in the design tokens file.
- The texture: `GridField`, the `band` fit, and the mirrored still.
- Shared ledger parts, so every ledger Block draws its rules and cells the same way.

### Content and props authors need to know about

- `eyebrow` is available on sixteen Blocks.
- Comparison takes labels for its two columns: `usLabel` and `traditionalLabel`.
- Faq takes an optional `contact` link for anyone whose question is not listed.
- Pricing takes at most 4 plans.
- The home page has a new composition.

### Docs

- The `design-a-block` skill was rewritten.
- The `compose-page` skill was updated.
- The /style-guide page was rewritten.

### Breaking or behavioural changes

- Pricing accepts at most 4 plans. A page with 5 or more plans fails the build.
- LogoCloud now requires `eyebrow`. A page that uses LogoCloud without it fails the build.
- LogoCloud no longer has a `lede` prop. Text passed as `lede` is ignored, so move it to `eyebrow` or `meta`.
- Hero's default `variant` is now `showcase`. A Hero that sets no `variant` used to render as `centered`, so set `variant="centered"` to keep the old look.
- Hero's `title` is now optional.
- Testimonial and Faq keep their old props. Both now draw differently, and Faq shows its first answer open.
- FeatureGrid and Team pad a short last row with empty cells. Use a multiple of 3 items.
- Gallery puts the 1st, 6th, 7th and 8th images in the wide slots, so the order of images matters.
